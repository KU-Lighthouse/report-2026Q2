Quarterly Report
Q2 2026

Key Performance Indicators April – June 2026 Nurture · Engage · Empower Internal — Management & Teams Data per 30 June 2026
UCPH Lighthouse enhances innovation and entrepreneurship across the university. We foster ideas and solutions for the benefit of people, society and the environment. This report covers UCPH Lighthouse's three activity tracks: Nurture, Engage and Empower.
Executive Summary

Q2 2026 — Key Learnings from UCPH Lighthouse

Nurture — Strong H1 pace on curricular reach and awareness, with Health and Social Sciences catching up. Students in curricular courses (754 H1, 87% of full-year 2025) and student awareness reach (1,003 H1, 70% of 2025 — though the Q2 jump is driven almost entirely by cross-faculty "UCPH Across" activity) are both tracking well. Health and Social Sciences, absent or minimal in Q1, both showed real Q2 activity. Educators engaged nearly doubled (102 → 192).
Engage — Events grew sharply; platform and programme metrics need a change-in-definition caveat. Event sign-ups nearly quadrupled (409 → 1,571, +284%, incl. Community Breakfast), and a new Researcher Outreach channel (561 VIPs reached across 19 activities) adds a previously untracked dimension. Student users/startup-projects dropped sharply (631→26, 411→11) due to a Q2 methodology change (active student users/projects only, not comparable to Q1). 3 of 10 programmes had uptake in Q2 with a total programme participation of 86 — including a new "Fintech Venture Track" running.
Empower — Licences and spin-outs recovered strongly; IDFs and PoC funding continued growing. Licences went from 2 to 10 and spin-outs from 0 to 4, addressing the Q1-low flagged last quarter. IDFs (92% of 2025 pace) and PoC funded (85% of 2025 pace) both grew quarter-on-quarter. Health and Science still dominate every stage, but Social Sciences entered the PoC pipeline for the first time in Q2.
